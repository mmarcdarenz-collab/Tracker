💡 How Smart Progression works

Daily Tracker checks your previous weight, reps and effort. If you still had reps left, it usually adds reps first. Once you reach the top of your target range with good form, it suggests the next weight.

🟢 Easy: 3+ reps left 🟡 Ideal: 1–3 reps left 🔴 Max: no clean reps left
Physique illustrations are visual examples only. Results also depend on nutrition, recovery, body-fat level and genetics.
